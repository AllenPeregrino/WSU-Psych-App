## Work Summary (Developer Facing)
Currently our main application is a Qualtrics based survey, due to the university not supporting Qualtrics anymore this is costing our client extra money. Our first challenge is to take our Qualtrics based survey and migrate it to REDCap as this application is supported by the university and won’t cost anything extra as well as follows information storage laws such as HIPPA. We have created a REDCap project and have gotten our API approved by the administration and completed our main project settings. 


## Unfinished Work
We are currently waiting for the client to provide the survey we will use in REDCap as there is no way to import the Qualtrics survey directly into Redcap. They are looking to assign this work to undergrad students. Once the survey is provided, we will be able to swap the Qualtrics API for the Redcap API for a seamless change in our application. We have already created a REDCap project that has been approved by the administration as well as our API being approved. We will be moving objective to sprint 2.
